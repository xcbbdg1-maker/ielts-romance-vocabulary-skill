## Teaching-unit selection

Prefer a complete phrase when the learner needs the whole pattern to use it correctly. Teach `comply with an order`, not an isolated `comply` plus an unreliable preposition guess. Label words and phrases accurately.

Use the meaning required by the scene, then check that the same meaning is reflected in the Chinese gloss, part-of-speech label, collocation, and error note. Do not make the story use a process while the explanation defines only its result.

## Pronunciation

- Choose British or American IPA and state the choice.
- Keep weak forms and stress marks readable rather than overloading a phrase with narrow phonetic detail.
- Verify silent letters and stress claims; do not infer them from spelling.
- For phrases, pronounce the phrase as a teaching aid but preserve the headword stress.
- Treat pronunciation claims as uncertain until checked against a reputable learner dictionary when accuracy matters.

## Formation and origin

Prefer transparent, useful relations:

- decide → decision
- respond → response
- regulate → regulation → regulatory

Distinguish synchronically useful formation from historical origin. Do not invent a “root” merely because two words look similar. If analysis is uncertain, write `整体记忆` and provide a reliable word family instead.

Explain spelling changes precisely. For example, `measure + -able` becomes `measurable`; do not claim that the final `e` is retained. Avoid simplistic grammar mnemonics such as “between is only for two.”

## Collocations

Choose a phrase a learner could reuse in IELTS speaking or writing. Prefer common verb–noun, adjective–noun, preposition, and countability patterns.

Reject combinations that are grammatically possible but pragmatically strange. Safer examples include:

- `launch a personal attack`, not forcing “人身攻击” into `personal accusation`;
- `tamper with the fire alarm`, not `manipulated fire alarm` when physical interference is meant;
- `the suspect’s identity remains unknown`, not `unresolved identity`;
- `open up the possibility of reconciliation`, not an incomplete `renewed possibility`;
- `save a backup copy` or `create a backup`; use `secure` only when protecting it;
- `shield somebody from falling debris`, not `protective action` for one bodily movement;
- `perform biometric matching` for the process; `confirm a biometric match` for the result.

Check the preposition as part of the unit:

- comply with an order;
- protect somebody from harm;
- reconcile A with B;
- be committed to doing something.

## Error notes

Add `易错` only when it prevents a plausible learner error. Good categories include pronunciation, stress, irregular spelling, inflection, countability, required preposition, false friends, register, process-versus-result distinctions, and spelling conventions.

Do not manufacture an error note for every item. A forced warning increases noise and can teach a false rule.

## Final linguistic audit

Review independently from plot quality:

1. Read only the English units and Chinese glosses.
2. Read only the collocations.
3. Read only formation and origin notes.
4. Read only error notes.
5. Check phrase type, countability, prepositions, hyphenation, spelling convention, and IPA consistency.
6. Replace an item rather than defending a forced use.

Mechanical checks cannot certify language quality. Do not describe an AI or heuristic review as official IELTS scoring or official dictionary validation.
